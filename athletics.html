Registration · Reporting · Competition · Valedictory

The 68th Kerala State School Sports Meet begins with district-level registration and reporting at multiple centres on 25–26 October 2026, followed by the state-level athletics competitions at the Maj. Dhyanchand Sports Complex, NIT Kozhikode from 27 to 31 October 2026, concluding with the valedictory function on 31 October at 3:00 PM.

Step 1 · Registration Player registration at reporting centres 25 Oct 2026 · 2:00 PM — Registration opens at NCC directorate and designated reporting centres for all participating sub-junior, junior, senior boys & girls and teachers' categories.
Step 2 · Reporting Reporting at competition centres 26 Oct 2026 · 6:30 – 7:30 PM — All athletes report at their respective venues (Attingal, Kondotty, Chathannoor, Kayamkulam, Kollam, Karunagappally, Punnapra-Vayalar, Cherthala, Central Stadium Thiruvananthapuram).
Step 3 · Competition State-level athletics competitions 27 – 30 Oct 2026 — Four full competition days with morning and evening sessions. Day 5 (31 Oct) hosts the closing sprints, finals, relays, cross-country and teachers' events.
Step 4 · Closing Valedictory function 31 Oct 2026 · 3:00 PM — Closing ceremony and prize distribution at the Maj. Dhyanchand Sports Complex, NIT, Kozhikode.

Important notes for athletes & officials

Athletes are permitted to use only spikes fitted with synthetic nails with a maximum length of 9 mm — with the sole exception of High Jump and Javelin Throw, where spikes up to 13 mm are allowed. All timings are in 24-hour format (hrs). Final events are flagged with a gold badge in the schedule. Heats and qualification rounds are marked accordingly.

Valedictory Function — 31 October 2026, 3:00 PM The 68th Kerala State School Sports Meet 2026 concludes with the valedictory function at the Maj. Dhyanchand Sports Complex, NIT, Kozhikode.

About the meet

The 68th Kerala State School Sports Meet 2026 is the state-level athletics championship for school students across Kerala, featuring sub-junior (U-14), junior (U-17) and senior (U-19) categories along with teachers' events.
